Meet the Coaches

Grant Shieh

Head Coach

Grant Shieh: Building Leaders in the Classroom and on the Court

For Grant Shieh, the transition from the history classroom at Lionville Middle School to the volleyball court is a natural one. Whether he’s guiding 8th graders through historical milestones or coaching the Downingtown boys' and girls' teams, his mission remains the same: helping young people find their confidence.


Grant’s own journey started in Bucks County, where he played as a libero for Council Rock North. He carried that passion to Temple University’s club team and eventually into the coaching world in 2022.

His Philosophy

Grant believes that sports are the ultimate classroom for "soft skills." While he’s focused on technical precision—a nod to his days as a libero—he’s even more dedicated to teaching:


  • Accountability: Owning your role on and off the court.
  • Resilience: Learning how to bounce back from a tough set or a difficult exam.
  • Collaboration: Understanding that the team is always stronger than the individual.

When the whistle blows and the school day ends, Grant stays busy. You might find him under the hood of his car, hiking a local trail, or losing his voice cheering for the Philadelphia sports scene. He’s a firm believer in a "lifelong appreciation for the game" and aims to ensure every athlete he coaches leaves the program as a better leader and a more well-rounded person.
