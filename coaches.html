Meet the Coaches

Grant Shieh

Head Coach

Building the Local Volleyball Community

For Grant Shieh, volleyball is more than a game—it’s the cornerstone of a thriving local community. While he spends his days as a history teacher at Lionville Middle School, his primary mission on the court is to elevate the standard of play and connection within the Downingtown area.

Grant currently leads both the Downingtown boys’ and girls’ programs, bringing a high-energy, community-first approach to every practice. His journey began in Bucks County as a libero for Council Rock North, continued through Temple University’s club team, and evolved into a full-time coaching career in 2022.


Coaching Philosophy

Grant’s coaching is built on the "Student-Athlete" Growth Model, emphasizing that sustainable success is the result of intentional, small shifts rather than lucky breakthroughs.

  • Community Impact: Creating a pipeline of talent where local players feel connected to their teammates and their town.
  • Incremental Growth: Breaking down complex skills into manageable steps to ensure players see consistent, measurable progress.
  • Confidence Through Sport: Using the court as a space where young athletes can test their limits and find their voice.
  • Accountability: Owning your role on and off the court.
  • Resilience: Learning how to bounce back from a tough set or a difficult exam.
  • Collaboration: Understanding that the team is always stronger than the individual.

When the whistle blows and the school day ends, Grant stays busy. You might find him under the hood of his car, hiking a local trail, or losing his voice cheering for the Philadelphia sports scene.

Chris D'Amore

Assistant Coach

Three Decades of Competitive Excellence

Born and raised in Texas, Chris D'Amore brings a wealth of knowledge and elite-level experience to Downingtown East. A graduate of Twin Valley and California University of PA, Chris has spent over 30 years immersed in the sport, developing a deep understanding of the technical and mental aspects of winning volleyball.


Chris’s extensive playing career spans from local dominance to the national collegiate stage:

  • Championship Pedigree: A former County Champion during his high school career at Twin Valley.
  • Collegiate Success: Played for a nationally ranked NCAA Division II program at California University of PA.
  • Active Competitor: Continues to compete at a high level, most recently with the Shamona Creek Volleyball team.

With a career rooted in high-stakes competition, Coach D'Amore is focused on instilling a championship mindset in our players, emphasizing the precision and discipline required to succeed at the next level.
