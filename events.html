How to Use the Events Calendar

This page is the one place to check what's coming up for Downingtown East Cougars boys volleyball. It lists open gyms, tryouts, practices, parent meetings, clinics and camps, and fundraisers, alongside every match on the schedule. Use the buttons at the top to show one kind of event, or switch to the month view to see the whole month at a glance.

Save an event to your phone

Every event has an Add to calendar option. Choose Google Calendar, or Apple / Outlook to download the event to the calendar app on your phone or computer. Events saved this way don't update themselves, so check back here if plans change.

Changes and cancellations

Times and locations can change, especially around weather and gym availability. This page is updated as soon as a change is made, and same-day changes are also posted on Instagram. Tryout details are posted in news ahead of the season. Questions? Contact the coaching staff.
