How the Downingtown East Boys Volleyball Season Works

Boys volleyball is a spring sport in Pennsylvania. Tryouts and first practices open with the PIAA spring season in early March, and the regular season runs March through May. The schedule mixes Ches-Mont League matches with non-league opponents, and those results set seeding for the PIAA District 1 tournament in May. District qualifiers advance to the PIAA state tournament in June. Preseason scrimmages are listed with a "Scrim" tag and don't count toward the season record.

Match format

Varsity matches are best of five sets. The first four sets are played to 25 points and a deciding fifth set to 15, each won by two. Junior varsity matches are commonly best of three. The PIAA volleyball rules guide covers scoring, time-outs, and the playoff path in full.
