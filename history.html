Downingtown East Boys Volleyball, Season by Season

This page is the record book for boys volleyball at Downingtown East High School in Exton, Pennsylvania. Each archived season below links to that year's full schedule and results, the roster that played it, and the recaps written along the way. Use it to look up a Downingtown East volleyball record for any year, find a former player's number and position, or trace how a match against a Ches-Mont opponent turned out.

The Cougars play in the Ches-Mont League and PIAA District 1. Boys volleyball is a spring sport in Pennsylvania, so a "season" here runs from tryouts in early March through the regular season in April and May, into the District 1 tournament in late May. District finishers who qualify advance to the PIAA state tournament in early June. A season's record on this page counts regular-season and playoff matches; preseason scrimmages are excluded.
