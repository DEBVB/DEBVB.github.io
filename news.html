DE opened the 2026 season at home against Wilson (Reading) in a five-set battle that showcased both the character and the potential of this group. Down 0–2 after the first two sets, the Cougars did something that matters — they didn’t fold. Sets 3 and 4 belonged to Downingtown East, forcing a deciding fifth set and showing real resolve under pressure.

Sets 3 & 4 were outstanding. Winning back-to-back sets against a strong Wilson program after falling behind 0–2 is a significant mental and physical achievement. The Cougars showed they can compete at this level when they’re locked in.

The fifth set didn’t go DE’s way, but this match tells a real story about a team with fight in it. Anish Yasa (#8) was the standout performer, leading the offense with efficiency and contributing in every phase of the game. The front row was active all night, putting up an impressive block total that disrupted Wilson’s rhythm throughout.

The Cougars head to Lansdale Catholic on Monday looking to carry the momentum from those middle sets into the next match. The foundation is there.

Next up: @ Lansdale Catholic — Monday, March 16, 5:00 PM
