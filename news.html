The Cougars brought the heat to the cross-town rivalry Monday night, taking down Downingtown West 3-1 in a hard-fought road victory. East dominated early, securing the first two sets with clinical execution before West clawed back in the third. The Cougars shut the door in the fourth, finishing with 43 total kills and a relentless defensive effort.

Efficiency at the net was key. Siddhanth Madipadaga (#28) put on a masterclass, hitting a staggering .438 with 9 kills. Meanwhile, Arjun Sankar (#13) orchestrated the attack with 35 assists while digging up 13 balls to keep plays alive.

Defensively, the back row was a wall. Ritvik Adoni (#7) and Parker LaVeyra (#4) led the charge with 18 digs each, ensuring West struggled to find the floor. LaVeyra also contributed significantly to the offensive pressure with 13 kills and led the team with 3 solo blocks. Sritan Bhumireddy (#1) paced the scoring with a team-high 15 kills.

The win moves the Cougars to 2-2 on the season, providing massive momentum as they dive deeper into the heart of the Ches-Mont schedule.
