Recent News

Parent Meeting — Friday After Practice

Posted on March 5, 2026

There will be a parent meeting in the library immediately following Friday's practice. All parents and guardians are encouraged to attend. A great opportunity to connect with the coaching staff, ask questions, and stay informed about the upcoming season.

JV Scorebook Clinic — Saturday After Practice

Posted on March 5, 2026

Following Saturday's practice (9:00–11:30 AM), all JV players are invited to stay for a "Learning the Book" clinic. Players will be taught how to keep the official scorebook during games — an important skill for supporting the team on match days.

Activity Fee Payment Reminder

Posted on March 5, 2026

All players are reminded to pay their activity fee as soon as possible if they have not done so. Fees can be paid through the school's athletic office. Reach out to Coach Shieh with any questions.

2026 Season Tryout Dates Announced

Posted on January 28, 2026

Tryouts for the 2026 season took place from March 2nd through March 4th. Rosters were finalized at the conclusion of Day 3. All prospective players must have their physical forms submitted to the athletic office before stepping onto the court.

Congratulations to Our Graduating Seniors!

Posted on June 5, 2025

A huge congratulations to the graduating class of 2025! We thank our senior players for their dedication, leadership, and hard work over the years. Best of luck in all your future endeavors!

Parent & Player Pre-Season Meeting

Posted on February 15, 2026

A mandatory pre-season meeting for all interested players and their parents was held on February 25th at 7:00 PM in the school cafeteria. The spring schedule and program expectations were discussed.
