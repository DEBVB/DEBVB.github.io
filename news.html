About Cougars Volleyball News

This page carries news from the Downingtown East boys volleyball program in Exton, Pennsylvania: match recaps with set scores and team stat lines, season announcements, and tryout information. During the season a recap goes up after each match. The newest post opens first, with earlier posts listed below it.

Boys volleyball is a spring sport in Pennsylvania, so news is busiest from March through May. Tryouts open in early March and are open to any Downingtown East student. The Cougars play in the Ches-Mont League and PIAA District 1.

For day-to-day updates, follow the team on Instagram @deastboysvb and X @devbboys. Every result is on the schedule, and recaps from past seasons are kept on the Program History page.
