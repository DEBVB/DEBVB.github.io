Meet the Coaches

Grant Shieh

Head Coach

Building the Local Volleyball Community

For Grant Shieh, volleyball is more than a game — it's the cornerstone of a thriving local community. A history teacher at Lionville Middle School, his primary mission on the court is to develop a high-level standard of play and cultivate a strong community within the Downingtown area.

Grant leads the Downingtown East boys' program and is assistant coach for the girls' program. His journey began as a libero for Council Rock North, continued through Temple University's club team, and into coaching at the high school and club level in 2021.

His coaching is built on the "Student-Athlete" Growth Model:

  • Community Impact: Creating a pipeline of talent where local players feel connected to teammates and their town.
  • Incremental Growth: Breaking down complex skills into manageable steps for consistent, measurable progress.
  • Confidence Through Sport: Using the court as a space where athletes can test their limits and find their voice.

Outside the gym you might find him under a car hood, hiking a local trail, skiing the Northeast, or cheering for Philadelphia sports.

Chris D'Amore

Assistant Coach

Three Decades of Competitive Excellence

Born and raised in Texas, Chris D'Amore brings elite-level experience to Downingtown East. A graduate of Twin Valley and California University of PA, Chris has spent over 30 years immersed in volleyball, developing a deep understanding of its technical and mental aspects.

His career spans local dominance to the national collegiate stage:

  • Championship Pedigree: County Champion during his high school career at Twin Valley.
  • Collegiate Success: Played for a nationally ranked NCAA Division II program at California University of PA.
  • Active Competitor: Continues to compete at a high level with the Shamona Creek Volleyball team.

Coach D'Amore focuses on instilling a championship mindset — emphasizing the precision and discipline required to succeed at the next level.
