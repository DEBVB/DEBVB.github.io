The Program

Downingtown East Boys Volleyball

Downingtown East Boys Volleyball is the boys' varsity and junior varsity volleyball program at Downingtown East High School in Exton, Chester County, Pennsylvania. Known as the Cougars, the team competes in the Ches-Mont League and in PIAA District 1, playing home matches in the Downingtown East gymnasium at 50 Devon Drive.

In Pennsylvania, boys volleyball is a spring sport. Tryouts open with the start of the PIAA spring season in early March, the regular season runs from March into May, and District 1 playoffs follow in late May, with qualifying teams advancing to the PIAA state tournament. Any Downingtown East student is welcome to try out — no club experience required. New to the sport in this area? Start with our guide to boys volleyball in Chester County.

The program is led by head coach Grant Shieh, with assistant coach Chris D'Amore. Beyond the high school season, the staff runs DEVB volleyball clinics for local players, and maintains a free volleyball score sheet guide for scorekeepers, parents, and student managers.
